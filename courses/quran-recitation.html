Course Details

Quran Recitation

A focused recitation course that helps students read more smoothly, more beautifully, and with stronger confidence through regular live correction.

Quran Recitation is for students who can already read but want to improve their flow, confidence, and listening accuracy. The teacher works closely with each student on fluency, pacing, repetition, stopping points, and the natural rhythm of tilawah.

What the student will learn

  • How to read with better fluency and steadiness
  • Proper stopping and starting in recitation
  • How to listen for and correct repeated reading mistakes
  • Daily habits that strengthen recitation quality

What the student will master

  • Smoother recitation with less hesitation
  • Better breath control and phrase balance
  • Improved confidence when reading aloud
  • Cleaner, more polished Quran delivery

Who this course is best for

This course suits children, teens, and adults who can read the Quran but want to sound more fluent and accurate.
