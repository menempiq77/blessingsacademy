Course Details

Quran Memorization

A personalized hifz program that balances new memorization, strong revision, and steady retention so progress stays stable over time.

Quran Memorization is built around realistic hifz planning. Each student receives a pace that matches age, ability, and schedule. Lessons focus on new memorization, revision of previous portions, listening accuracy, and teacher correction so the memorized verses stay strong and not temporary.

What the student will learn

  • How to memorize with a realistic and repeatable method
  • How to divide time between new hifz and revision
  • Ways to strengthen weak passages through repetition
  • How to maintain retention with teacher accountability

What the student will master

  • Better memorization discipline and daily routine
  • More stable recall of memorized surahs and ayat
  • Improved revision habits that prevent quick forgetting
  • Greater confidence when reciting memorized portions

Who this course is best for

This course is suitable for children beginning hifz, students returning after a gap, and adults who want a steady memorization plan.
