Aqidah Course Levels

Level 1

Students will discover the fundamental foundations of Islamic faith. They will learn about Allah, the One and Only, by exploring the Oneness of Lordship (Tawhid ar-Rububiyyah), Worship (Tawhid al-Uluhiyyah), and the Divine Names and Attributes. They will also study belief in the angels, their roles, and their importance in the divine message.

Level 2

Students will understand the role of the divine scriptures as sources of guidance, their preservation, and their replacement by the Quran as the final revelation. They will also deepen their understanding of the mission of the prophets, their exemplary character, and their role in calling people to the Oneness of Allah.

Level 3

Students will study belief in divine destiny (Qadar), covering its essential dimensions such as Allah's knowledge and will, predestination, and human responsibility. This level also introduces the realities of the Hereafter, including resurrection, judgment, and the first stages after death, such as life in the grave, with its rewards or punishments.

Level 4

Students will focus on the major events of the Last Day, including resurrection, judgment, the bridge (As-Sirat), the basin (Al-Hawd), and the scale (Al-Mizan). They will also explore the realities of Paradise and Hell, understanding their role in divine justice, the ultimate reward for believers, and the punishment for sinners.

Who this course is best for

This course is ideal for anyone who wishes to understand the foundations of Islamic belief and strengthen their faith.
