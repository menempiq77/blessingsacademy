Course Details

Islamic Studies

A balanced Islamic Studies course that teaches belief, worship, character, and prophetic guidance in a clear and age-appropriate way.

Islamic Studies helps students grow in understanding as well as practice. Lessons can include aqeedah, fiqh, seerah, adab, du'as, and core Islamic values, all taught in a way that matches the student's age and level. The aim is to connect knowledge with daily life.

What the student will learn

  • Foundations of belief and worship
  • Important fiqh topics for daily practice
  • Seerah lessons and prophetic character
  • Islamic manners, du'as, and values

What the student will master

  • Clearer understanding of key Islamic topics
  • Better daily practice with confidence
  • Stronger Islamic manners and awareness
  • A healthier connection between learning and action

Who this course is best for

This course is ideal for children building a strong Islamic foundation and for families who want regular structured guidance.
