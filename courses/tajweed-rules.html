Course Details

Tajweed Rules

A structured Tajweed course that teaches the rules clearly and then trains the student to apply them correctly during actual recitation.

Tajweed Rules is for students who want to move beyond basic reading and understand how Quran recitation should be pronounced properly. Lessons cover the major rules in an organized way, with examples, repetition, and practical application from the Mushaf.

What the student will learn

  • Makharij and key articulation points
  • Rules such as noon sakinah, meem sakinah, madd, and qalqalah
  • How to identify Tajweed rules inside Quran verses
  • How to apply the rules during live recitation

What the student will master

  • Stronger pronunciation with fewer repeated errors
  • Recognition of common Tajweed patterns in the Quran
  • Practical use of rules while reading aloud
  • A more accurate and disciplined style of recitation

Who this course is best for

This course is ideal for students who can already read but want to improve precision and recitation accuracy.
